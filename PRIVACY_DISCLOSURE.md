## ENGLISH VERSION

### What We Collect

The Lucide React application collects different types of information to provide services and improve your experience:

#### 1. Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number
- **Financial Records**: Transactions, bank accounts, rental agreements, tax documents
- **Documents**: Invoices (NF-e, NFS-e), receipts, contracts, bank statements
- **Preferences**: Language, timezone, UI customization

#### 2. Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: Features accessed, screens viewed, actions performed
- **Session Data**: Session duration, frequency of use, activity patterns
- **Device Information**: Operating system, app version, device type
- **Technical Data**: IP address (anonymized), timestamp of activities
- **Performance Data**: App load times, feature response times, memory usage
- **Crash Reports**: Error messages, stack traces, app state at crash time (if enabled)

#### 3. Why We Collect It

| Data Type | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Account Information | Account management, authentication, communication | Contract (service provision) |
| Financial Records | Provide accounting services, tax compliance | Contract + Legal obligation (CTN) |
| Documents | Support financial reconciliation, evidence of transactions | Contract |
| Usage Analytics | Improve app features, understand user needs, optimize performance | User consent |
| Crash Reports | Debug errors, improve stability, fix bugs | User consent |
| Device Information | Ensure app compatibility, security monitoring | Legitimate interest |

### How We Use Your Data

#### 1. Service Delivery
- **Account Management**: Create and maintain your account, verify your identity
- **Financial Services**: Record transactions, reconcile accounts, generate reports

- **Fraud Prevention**: Detect unusual activity, prevent unauthorized access
- **Account Protection**: Monitor for security threats, protect financial data

### Your Rights and Choices

#### 1. Right to Know What We Collect
- **Access Your Data**: Settings → Privacy & Compliance → Export My Data
- **Download Format**: Get all your data in machine-readable format (JSON)
- **Frequency**: Once per 30 days free; additional requests may have fees
- **Timeline**: Export available immediately for download

#### 2. Right to Correct Inaccurate Data
- **Fix Account Info**: Settings → Account → Edit Profile
- **Fix Transaction Data**: Click transaction, edit category/notes, save
- **Fix Wrong Amounts**: Edit transaction and correct the amount
- **Timeline**: Changes effective immediately
- **Support**: If you need help, contact privacy@lucidereact.app

#### 3. Right to Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")
- **Request Deletion**: Settings → Privacy & Compliance → Delete Account
- **How It Works**:
  1. You request deletion
  2. 30-day notice period (you can cancel during this time)
  3. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted
  4. Financial records required by law retained per tax requirements
- **What Gets Deleted**: Account, transactions, documents, analytics, crash reports
- **What Stays**: Financial records (required by law for 5-10 years)
- **Timeline**: Permanent deletion after 30 days

#### 4. Right to Restrict Processing
- **Pause Analytics**: Settings → Privacy → Analytics (toggle OFF)
- **Disable Crash Reporting**: Settings → Privacy → Crash Reporting (toggle OFF)
- **Disable Personalization**: Settings → Privacy → Personalization (toggle OFF)
- **Effect**: Data collection pauses; existing data kept per retention schedule
- **Timeline**: Changes effective immediately

#### 5. Right to Withdraw Consent
- **Anytime**: You can withdraw consent for any feature
- **Method**: Settings → Privacy → Consent Management (toggle OFF)
- **Effect**: Feature stops collecting data from that moment forward
- **Retroactive**: Withdrawal does NOT delete data already collected (see Deletion)

#### 6. Right to Data Portability
- **Export Your Data**: Settings → Privacy & Compliance → Export My Data
- **Format**: JSON, CSV, PDF available
- **Content**: All personal data associated with your account
- **Frequency**: Available immediately, once per 30 days free

#### 7. Right to Object
- **Opt Out**: You can opt out of any processing
- **Marketing**: Unsubscribe from promotional emails (one-click in email footer)
- **Analytics**: Disable analytics in Settings → Privacy
- **Contact**: Email privacy@lucidereact.app to object to specific processing

#### 8. Right to Appeal
- **Disagree With Decision**: If you disagree with our handling of your data, contact privacy@lucidereact.app
- **Complaint to Authority**: You can file complaint with data protection authority
  - **Brazil**: ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados)
  - **EU**: Your country's Data Protection Authority
