## Communication Strategy Overview

### Pillars of Communication

**1. Transparency**
- Share progress openly
- Acknowledge limitations honestly
- Communicate issues proactively

**2. Customer-Centric**
- Focus on customer benefits, not features
- Address customer pain points
- Provide education and enablement

**3. Accessibility**
- Multiple communication channels
- Clear, simple language
- Localized to 40+ languages

**4. Engagement**
- Two-way communication
- Encourage feedback
- Create community

### Communication Goals

**Awareness:** 500,000+ aware of Lucide launch
**Consideration:** 100,000+ download and try app
**Adoption:** 50,000+ become active monthly users
**Advocacy:** 10,000+ share Lucide with others
**Revenue:** Convert 5% to paid tier

### Channel Strategy

| Channel | Purpose | Frequency | Owner |

## In-App Notification Strategy

### Notification Hierarchy

**Critical (Red):** System issues, security alerts
**Important (Orange):** Feature updates, breaking changes
**Standard (Blue):** Tips, feature highlights, activity
**Informational (Gray):** Best practices, reminders

### Launch Day Notifications

**On First Launch (Day 0):**
```
Welcome to Lucide! 👋

Ready to create your first diagram? Let's get started with a quick tour.

[Start Tour] [Skip]
```

**After Tutorial Complete:**
```
You're all set! ✨

Here's what you can do next:
• Invite team members to collaborate
• Try a template from our library
• Watch 2-minute tips & tricks video

[Invite Team] [Browse Templates] [Watch Video]
```

### Ongoing Notifications

**Daily (Smart Timing):**
- Feature tips (one per day, random)
- Teammate activity in shared diagrams
- Collaboration invitations

**Weekly:**
- Digest of team activity
- Popular diagrams this week
- Feature recommendation based on usage

**As-Needed:**
- Payment issues
- Plan upgrade recommendations
- New team member joined
- New comment on your diagram

### Notification Preferences

**User Controls:**
- Turn off category of notifications
- Set quiet hours (no notifications 10pm-7am)
- Customize digest frequency
- Choose notification channels (in-app, email, push)

**Recommended Settings (Default):**
- Push notifications: ON
- Email notifications: Digest only (1x/week)
- In-app notifications: ON
- Quiet hours: 10pm-7am (user's timezone)

## FAQ & Common Concerns

### General Questions

**Q: Is Lucide free?**
A: Lucide has a generous free tier with unlimited diagrams. Pro ($11.99/month) adds advanced features. Enterprise plans available for large teams.

**Q: What platforms does Lucide support?**
A: Currently iOS (14+) and Android (11+). Web app and desktop apps coming in Q1 2027.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. We use AES-256 encryption, SOC 2 certification, and comply with GDPR and CCPA.

**Q: Can I import my diagrams from other tools?**
A: Yes. Lucide imports Visio (.vsdx), Draw.io, and other formats with 90%+ accuracy.

### Pricing Questions

**Q: Can I try Pro for free?**
A: Yes. New users get 14 days free. After trial, Pro is $11.99/month or $99/year (save 30%).

**Q: Is there a family or student discount?**
A: Student accounts get Pro free. Family plans available for $19.99/month (up to 5 family members).

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
A: Yes. Cancel instantly with no questions asked. Your diagrams remain viewable in free tier.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my storage limit?**
A: Pro tier has unlimited storage. Free tier: 5GB storage included (rare to exceed).

### Technical Questions

**Q: Will my diagrams sync across devices?**
A: Automatically. Changes sync to cloud in seconds. Offline changes sync when online.

**Q: Can I export my diagrams?**
A: Absolutely. Export to PNG, SVG, PDF, PowerPoint, Excel, or our native format.

**Q: What if I lose connection while editing?**
A: Your changes are saved locally. Sync happens automatically when connection returns.

**Q: How large can a diagram be?**
A: Up to 100MB file size, 10,000+ shapes. For larger, consider splitting into sub-diagrams.

### Collaboration Questions

**Q: How many people can edit a diagram at once?**
A: Up to 100 concurrent editors per diagram.

**Q: Can I control who can see my diagrams?**
A: Yes. Fine-grained permissions: View only, Can comment, Can edit, Can manage.

**Q: What happens if two people edit the same shape?**
A: Lucide automatically merges changes. Conflicts are rare and resolved intelligently.

**Q: Can I track who made what changes?**
A: Yes. Activity log shows timestamp, user, and exactly what changed. Revert anytime.

### Billing Questions

**Q: When will I be charged?**
A: Billing is monthly (every 30 days from purchase) or yearly (at renewal date). No hidden charges.

**Q: Can I change my plan anytime?**
A: Yes. Upgrade/downgrade effective immediately. Pro-rated refunds for downgrades.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal.

**Q: Do I get an invoice?**
A: Yes. Automatic invoices emailed monthly.

### Support Questions

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: In-app chat (Settings → Help), email (support@lucide.app), or forum (forum.lucide.app).

**Q: What are support response times?**
A: Free: 24 hours | Pro: 4 hours | Enterprise: 1 hour (24/7).

**Q: Is there a community forum?**
A: Yes. forum.lucide.app is active with peer support and Lucide team participation.
